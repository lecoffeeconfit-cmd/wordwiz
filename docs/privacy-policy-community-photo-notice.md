# Privacy policy addition: Community profile photos

Add this section to the published WordWiz Privacy Policy before releasing Community profile-photo uploads:

> ## Community profile photos and safety screening
> If you choose a Community profile photo, WordWiz asks for your permission before sending that photo to OpenAI solely to screen it for harmful content. The photo is saved for your Community profile only when it passes that safety check. OpenAI processes the photo under its own applicable policies. You can choose not to upload a photo and still use WordWiz.

The in-app first-upload notice uses the same disclosure and requires the learner to choose **Continue** before the image picker opens.
